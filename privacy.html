Privacy Policy

Focal Point Massage Therapy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Information We Collect

When you use our online booking system, we may collect information including your name, email address, phone number, appointment details, and information you voluntarily provide when scheduling an appointment.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule and manage appointments, communicate with you regarding your appointments, provide customer service, and send appointment-related notifications when you have consented to receive them.

SMS/Text Messaging

Clients may choose to opt in to receive automated transactional text messages from Focal Point Massage Therapy regarding their appointments. These messages may include appointment reminders and information related to an existing appointment.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing or receiving massage services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages or HELP for help.

Mobile Information

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS consent information, will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to help operate our booking, communication, and appointment-reminder systems. Information may be provided to these service providers only as necessary to provide those services on our behalf.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information provided through our website and booking system.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact Focal Point Massage Therapy through our website.
